Subject: What new customers are telling us about onboarding

Hana, Sam,

Notes from 9 onboarding calls this month:

- Connecting the first data source is the #1 blocker. Postgres users get stuck on SSL mode and our IP allowlist
  (the IPs are buried in a help article).
- Invite emails land in spam for Outlook/M365 customers, so the admin thinks teammates ignored the invite.
- After connecting data, they see an empty workspace. No templates, no sample dashboard, so "now what?"
- Two customers didn't realize Starter has no API access until after they bought.

Activation this month is 34%. I suspect these are the main reasons.
