# Definition

**NRR** = (ARR from customers who existed 12 months ago, measured today) ÷ (their ARR 12 months ago).

Includes expansion, contraction and churn from that cohort. Excludes revenue from new customers.

# Reporting

- Calculated monthly on the first business day, from the billing system snapshot.
- Reported as a percentage with one decimal (e.g. 112.4%).
- Target for FY2026: **≥ 110%**.
