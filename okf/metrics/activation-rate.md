# Definition

**Activation Rate** = new workspaces that are *activated* within 14 days of signup ÷ all new workspaces
created in the period.

A workspace is **activated** when it has (a) connected at least one data source **and**
(b) at least one user who counts toward [MAU](/metrics/monthly-active-users.md) (i.e. did a qualifying action).

# Reporting

Weekly cohorts, reported every Monday. Test and internal workspaces are excluded.
