# Policy

**Annual plans:** a customer can get a **full refund within 30 days** of the initial purchase
**or** of the annual renewal date. After 30 days, no refunds are given; the plan runs to the end of the term.

**Monthly plans:** no refunds. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

**Exception, service credits:** if an outage breaches the SLA (Enterprise only, 99.9% monthly uptime),
the customer receives service credits, not a cash refund.

# Process

1. Support confirms the purchase/renewal date in the billing system.
2. Refunds inside the window are approved by Finance Ops within 5 business days.
3. Anything outside the policy requires written approval from the Finance Ops lead.

# History

Approved by Finance Ops on 2026-04-15. This policy replaces the earlier draft proposals discussed in
Q1 2026 meetings (14-day and 60-day windows). Neither of those was adopted.
