# Retention periods

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Cached query results | 30 days |
| Audit logs | 1 year (Enterprise: 3 years) |
| Deleted workspaces | Recoverable for 14 days, then permanently purged |
| Backups | 35 days |

Customers on Enterprise can request deletion of all data within 30 days of contract end.
