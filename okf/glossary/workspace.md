# Definition

A **workspace** is one customer account in Northwind. It holds data sources, dashboards, reports and users.
Billing, plan limits and [API rate limits](/api/rate-limits.md) are all applied **per workspace**.

A company can have several workspaces (e.g. one per region), each billed separately.
Workspace IDs look like `ws_4410`.
